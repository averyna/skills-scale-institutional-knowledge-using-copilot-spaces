# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **QA/Testing Leads** on acceptance criteria, test coverage, and automation
- Work with **Technical Leads/Architects** on design decisions and technical approach
- Partner with **Product Managers** to clarify requirements and priorities
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on timeline and dependency management

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Align with **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on business objectives and success metrics
- Work with **Project Managers** on release planning and roadmap communication
- Collaborate with **Developers** and **Technical Leads/Architects** on feasibility and design trade-offs
- Partner with **QA/Testing Leads** on acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Engage **UX/Design Leads** on user experience and design validation

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Report to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on progress, risks, and decisions
- Partner with **Product Managers** on roadmap and milestone alignment
- Coordinate with **Developers** and **Technical Leads/Architects** on technical dependencies and timelines
- Collaborate with **QA/Testing Leads** on quality and release readiness
- Work with **Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches** on ceremony facilitation and process improvements
- Engage **DevOps/Operations Engineers** on deployment planning and infrastructure readiness

---

## Stakeholders/Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors are business decision-makers and champions who provide business context, approve priorities and budgets, and define success metrics. They are invested in project outcomes and responsible for ensuring alignment between technical delivery and business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project priorities and scope trade-offs
- Provide feedback on feature releases and demos
- Escalate risks and blockers affecting project success
- Communicate outcomes to broader leadership

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between technical delivery and business objectives
- Remove organizational blockers and barriers
- Enable sustainable project execution and team capacity

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and milestone reviews
- Monthly stakeholder briefings and executive reports
- Ad-hoc escalations and decision approvals
- Demo reviews and feedback sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with **Product Managers** to define business value and success criteria
- Receive updates from **Project Managers** on progress and risk status
- Attend demos and reviews facilitated by **Developers** and **QA/Testing Leads**
- Provide guidance and remove blockers impacting the delivery team

---

## QA/Testing Leads

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads own quality standards, test strategy, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure features meet quality requirements and work across the team to reduce defects before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans for each feature and release
- Establish acceptance criteria standards and validate against them
- Coordinate and execute manual testing and QA sign-off
- Partner with developers on test coverage and automation
- Document known issues and quality metrics
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Validate release readiness before production deployment

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce bugs and rework in production
- Provide clear quality signals to the team and stakeholders
- Build confidence in release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria review
- Quality metrics and test status in standups and demos
- Test plans and QA reports
- Post-release quality assessments

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** on test automation and coverage strategies
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and quality thresholds
- Work with **Project Managers** on QA timeline and release gates
- Provide quality signals to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on release readiness
- Coordinate with **DevOps/Operations Engineers** on production validation and monitoring

---

## Technical Leads/Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical design decisions, manage technical debt, and ensure the team has the support and clarity needed to deliver quality solutions. They own architectural decisions and serve as escalation points for complex technical challenges.

### Responsibilities
- Make or guide technical design decisions for major features and systems
- Review and mentor on architectural patterns and best practices
- Manage technical debt and refactoring work
- Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies
- Provide technical guidance and support to the development team
- Ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and secure

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver quality solutions efficiently
- Reduce technical risk and build maintainability
- Support career development and knowledge sharing
- Balance feature delivery with technical health

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical discussions
- Architecture documentation and decisions
- Mentoring and code review feedback
- Technical risk escalation and mitigation planning

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Developers** on design reviews and technical decisions
- Partner with **Product Managers** on feasibility and trade-offs
- Work with **Project Managers** on technical timeline and dependency management
- Coordinate with **QA/Testing Leads** on testability and quality approaches
- Engage with **Security/Compliance Leads** on security and compliance requirements
- Support **DevOps/Operations Engineers** on deployment and operational concerns

---

## Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team ceremonies, remove process blockers, and help teams optimize their delivery practices. They are servant leaders who support the team's ability to execute and improve continuously.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove impediments and blockers
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track and communicate team metrics (velocity, cycle time, burndown)
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration
- Support adoption of agile practices across the organization

### Goals
- Enable the team to execute efficiently and sustainably
- Continuously improve processes and team performance
- Foster a culture of collaboration, feedback, and learning
- Reduce cycle time and increase predictability

### Typical Communication
- Ceremony facilitation and meeting notes
- Blocker escalation and resolution tracking
- Team health and retrospective action items
- Process improvement recommendations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Support **Developers** with sprint planning and impediment resolution
- Work with **Project Managers** on schedule coordination and planning
- Coach **Product Managers** on backlog refinement and prioritization
- Facilitate communication across all roles
- Escalate cross-team blockers to **Project Managers** and **Stakeholders/Sponsors**

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business requirements into clear, actionable specifications that developers can implement. They bridge the gap between business stakeholders and the technical delivery team, ensuring requirements are understood and feasible.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Translate requirements into detailed specifications and user stories
- Validate requirements with stakeholders and clarify ambiguities
- Identify impacts, dependencies, and trade-offs
- Support acceptance criteria definition and validation
- Document business processes and decision logic

### Goals
- Ensure clear, unambiguous requirements are delivered to the team
- Reduce rework and scope creep through thorough analysis
- Enable alignment between business needs and technical solutions
- Improve requirements quality and team efficiency

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specifications
- Stakeholder interviews and clarification sessions
- Collaboration with developers on feasibility
- Acceptance criteria and definition of done validation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Stakeholders/Sponsors** to understand business needs
- Work with **Product Managers** on requirements prioritization and clarification
- Collaborate with **Developers** and **Technical Leads/Architects** on feasibility and design
- Support **QA/Testing Leads** in defining acceptance criteria
- Provide guidance to **Project Managers** on requirement complexity and dependencies

---

## UX/Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX and Design Leads own user experience strategy, design standards, and usability. They ensure features are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with brand and user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define user experience strategy and design standards
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Collaborate with developers on implementation and feasibility
- Ensure accessibility and inclusive design standards are met
- Iterate on designs based on feedback and metrics

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce support costs through great design
- Validate design decisions with data and user feedback
- Build brand consistency and user confidence

### Typical Communication
- Design specs and component libraries
- User research findings and insights
- Prototype reviews and feedback sessions
- Accessibility and usability reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on feature priorities and user value
- Work with **Developers** on implementation and design feasibility
- Partner with **QA/Testing Leads** on usability testing
- Support **Stakeholders/Sponsors** with user-centered value demonstrations
- Provide guidance to **Business Analysts** on user workflows and needs

---

## Security/Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure projects meet security requirements, regulatory standards, and organizational policies. They provide guidance on secure design and identify security risks throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for projects
- Review designs and implementations for security risks
- Ensure compliance with applicable regulations and standards
- Conduct security assessments and threat modeling
- Advise on secure coding practices and architectural patterns
- Support incident response and remediation efforts

### Goals
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and compliance risks
- Enable secure, compliant product delivery
- Build customer confidence and protect brand reputation
- Support regulatory and organizational requirements

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements and compliance checklists
- Security design reviews and threat assessments
- Risk assessment and remediation plans
- Compliance audit reports and certifications

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Technical Leads/Architects** on secure design decisions
- Partner with **Developers** on secure coding practices
- Support **Product Managers** on security feature requirements
- Work with **DevOps/Operations Engineers** on secure deployment and monitoring
- Advise **Project Managers** on security-related risks and timelines
- Provide security context to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on risk status

---

## DevOps/Operations Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps and Operations Engineers manage deployment infrastructure, production systems, and operational reliability. They enable fast, safe releases and maintain system health and performance.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment infrastructure and pipelines
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting systems
- Manage production systems and infrastructure capacity
- Support rapid, safe deployments and rollbacks
- Optimize system performance and cost
- Participate in incident response and post-incident reviews

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments with minimal risk
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce deployment cycle time and toil
- Support sustainable, observable systems

### Typical Communication
- Deployment automation and pipeline documentation
- Infrastructure and monitoring dashboards
- Deployment coordination and release notes
- Incident response and post-incident reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** on deployment practices and infrastructure needs
- Support **Project Managers** on deployment planning and release coordination
- Work with **QA/Testing Leads** on production validation and monitoring
- Collaborate with **Technical Leads/Architects** on scalability and reliability
- Coordinate with **Security/Compliance Leads** on secure deployment and compliance
- Report system health and performance to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** and **Product Managers**
